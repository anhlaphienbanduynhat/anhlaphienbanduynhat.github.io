  <h1>About Quiztide</h1>
  <p><strong>Quiztide</strong> is an independent digital publication dedicated to interactive self-discovery, personality frameworks, and curiosity-driven psychometric assessments.</p>

  <h2>Our Mission</h2>
  <p>The modern web is filled with sluggish, ad-bloated quiz platforms that freeze your phone and overwhelm you with popups. We built Quiztide to be the exact opposite:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Ultra-Fast & Mobile-First:</strong> Clean, minimalist code that loads in milliseconds with zero annoying interruptions.</li>
    <li><strong>Insightful & Objective:</strong> Assessments based on recognized archetypes, psychology models, and relatable behavioral dilemmas.</li>
    <li><strong>Private & Respectful:</strong> No mandatory logins, no tracking walls, and no paywalls.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Editorial Standards</h2>
  <p>Every assessment on Quiztide is thoughtfully authored and verified to ensure balanced scoring, relatable questions, and constructive, grounded outcomes.</p>
