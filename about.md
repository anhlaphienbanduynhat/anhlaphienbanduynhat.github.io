  <h1>About Quiztide</h1>
  <p><strong>Quiztide</strong> is an independent digital publication dedicated to interactive self-discovery, personality frameworks, and curiosity-driven trivia.</p>

  <h2>Our Mission</h2>
  <p>The modern web is filled with sluggish, ad-bloated quiz platforms that freeze your phone and overwhelm you with popups. We built Quiztide to be the exact opposite:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Ultra-Fast & Mobile-First:</strong> Clean code that loads in milliseconds with zero annoying interruptions.</li>
    <li><strong>Insightful & Fun:</strong> Quizzes based on recognized archetypes, psychology models, and relatable lifestyle dilemmas.</li>
    <li><strong>Shareable:</strong> Easy one-click sharing so you can compare your traits with friends and family.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Editorial Standards</h2>
  <p>Every quiz on Quiztide is thoughtfully written and tested to ensure balanced scoring, relatable questions, and uplifting, constructive results.</p>
