✨ What Is afflatus?

afflatus is a studio application for people who want to make and manage AI-powered pictures and videos. It gives you a friendly place to collect images, build video scenes, and arrange everything on a timeline so your project has a clear beginning, middle and end.

The studio is self-hosted. That means it is designed to run on your own computer and in front of your own ComfyUI setup, instead of sending your work to a stranger's website. Your images, clips and project files stay with you.

You do not need to be a programmer to use afflatus. You open the studio, you see your pictures, you drag them into a timeline, and you shape them into something you want to share. The heavy thinking about how the application is built stays out of your way.

🧰 What You Can Do With afflatus

🖼️ Picture Studio

Generate and collect images in one library. Turn a written idea into a picture, then keep the results you like side by side so you can compare them without hunting through folders.

🎞️ Video From Pictures

Take a still image and let the studio turn it into motion. This is useful for short scenes, gentle camera moves, and small animated moments that would take hours to build by hand.

🎬 Timeline Editor

Arrange clips in order on a visual timeline. Move a scene earlier, stretch a scene longer, and preview how the whole piece flows before you export it.

⚙️ Your Own ComfyUI

afflatus sits in front of the ComfyUI installation you already run. Your models, your settings and your workflow stay where you put them, and the studio simply gives you a clearer window into them.

🔒 Self-Hosted Privacy

Because the studio runs on your machine, your unfinished ideas and rough experiments do not need to leave your computer. You decide what to keep and what to share.

🧭 Your First Steps Inside the Studio

Once the window is open, the studio is arranged into areas you will recognise quickly. Start slowly and explore one area at a time.

Create a project. Give it a name you will remember, such as the idea you are working on. A project holds your pictures, your clips and your timeline in one place.

Make your first picture. Describe what you want to see in plain words. The more specific you are about the subject, the light and the mood, the closer the result will be to the picture in your head.

Save the ones you like. Try a few variations. Keeping several versions in your library is normal and useful, because a version you skip today may be the one you want tomorrow.

Add motion. Send a finished picture to the video side of the studio and let it become a short moving scene.

Build the timeline. Drop your scenes onto the timeline in the order you want them to appear. Move them around until the sequence feels right, then preview the whole thing from the start.

Export when you are happy. When the preview looks the way you imagined, save the finished result and share it however you like.

🔗 How afflatus Works With Your ComfyUI

Think of ComfyUI as the engine room and afflatus as the tidy front room. The engine room does the hard work of generating pictures and motion. The front room is where you look at results, compare options and arrange your scenes without needing to understand every wire and dial behind the wall.

Because both parts live on your own machine, nothing about your setup is decided by somebody else. You choose which models you keep, and you decide how much of your library to keep on disk.

If you are new to this way of working, the simplest habit is to start your ComfyUI setup first, then open afflatus. That order avoids most of the small connection hiccups people meet on their first day.
