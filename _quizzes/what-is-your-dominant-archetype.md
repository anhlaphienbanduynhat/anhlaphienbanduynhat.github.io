## Understanding Archetypes

Archetypes are universal, inherited patterns of thought and behavior originally popularized by psychiatrist Carl Jung. Rather than boxing you into a rigid category, discovering your dominant archetype helps you recognize your natural cognitive instincts and where your greatest leverage lies.

### How to use your result
* **Honor your core drive:** Recognize what genuinely energizes you and build your daily habits around it.
* **Watch out for your blind spots:** Leaders must guard against impatience, Creators against unfinished drafts, Sages against over-analysis, and Guardians against burnout.
